In Blender select RECIPE CONTROLS · select to tune, then edit its Custom Properties. Change recipe, spacing, tool dimensions, density, and jitter. Each ink also exposes its rotation in the modifier.

The three Tool objects are editable footprints. Unhide one to change its mesh. Saved-scene export keeps your edits; it does not rebuild the graph.
