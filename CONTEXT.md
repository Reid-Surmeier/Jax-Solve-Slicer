# Painting vocabulary

**Alpha plate**: a continuous coverage target for one ordered ink layer.

**Alpha stack**: the ordered plates and matching palette metadata.

**Mark recipe**: the editable rules that turn a plate into repeated marks.

**Tool footprint**: the shape and dimensions left by a painting tool. A digital footprint is not physically calibrated until measured.

**Coverage mark**: a placed impression whose shape, size, rotation and overlap contribute to the image.

**Layered SVG**: vector marks grouped in the order of their ink plates.

**Mark build**: a progressive reveal of an existing layered SVG.

**Color reconstruction**: a predicted composite of the ordered plates under a stated color model.

**Recipe control**: an exposed value that changes a recipe without rewriting its construction rules.
